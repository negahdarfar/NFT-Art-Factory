# Art Direction Bible — NFT Art Factory

## Mission
Build a recognizable body of original digital art with emotional clarity, visual restraint, strong authorship and durable collecting value. The objective is artistic distinction and a trustworthy collector experience—not artificial hype or guaranteed financial returns.

## Collection identity: The Archive of Silence
**Core question:** What remains meaningful after the world becomes quiet?

**Emotional territory:** absence, memory, fragile hope, abandoned technology, witness, renewal and the persistence of life.

**Visual grammar**
- One dominant symbolic object per composition.
- A readable silhouette and focal hierarchy at thumbnail size.
- Cinematic negative space; avoid filling every empty area.
- Restrained palettes with one deliberate accent color.
- Tactile material detail: patina, dust, weathering, paper, metal, glass and natural textures.
- Surrealism should clarify the metaphor, not become a pile of unrelated symbols.

**Composition rules**
1. Establish one primary focal point and, at most, one supporting symbol.
2. Preserve clear separation between subject and background.
3. Check legibility at  thumbnail, phone and full-screen sizes.
4. Avoid accidental text, pseudo-logos, malformed lettering and decorative noise.
5. Keep a consistent visual language without repeating the same central metaphor.

## Collection architecture
Each work must have:
- A unique title and stable identifier.
- A one-sentence concept.
- A distinct visual metaphor.
- A concise collector-facing description.
- A documented creation/provenance record.
- A rights and owner-review state.
- A final master asset and a delivery derivative, when applicable.

## Curation rubric
Score each dimension from 1–5 during human review. Scores are editorial aids, not objective truth.

| Dimension | Review question |
|---|---|
| Concept clarity | Can the central idea be understood without a long explanation? |
| Originality | Does the metaphor feel specific rather than generic? |
| Composition | Is the focal hierarchy immediately clear? |
| Craft | Are edges, anatomy, materials, lighting and fine details coherent? |
| Emotional resonance | Does the image create a memorable feeling or question? |
| Collection fit | Does it belong to the series without duplicating another work? |
| Thumbnail strength | Does it remain recognizable at small sizes? |
| Technical readiness | Is the master file clean, correctly sized and documented? |

A high average never overrides a critical defect, unclear rights, missing provenance or absent owner approval.

## The Last Listener — art-direction note
The preferred candidate is variant 02: a square composition centered on a weathered shortwave radio and a small green shoot. Its strength is the contrast between a fading signal and new life. Preserve the simplicity; do not add extra symbols unless a deliberate revision improves the focal hierarchy.

## Rejection criteria
Reject or revise work with copied compositions, unlicensed source material, misleading provenance, unresolved rights, near-duplicate concepts, illegible focal points, obvious generation artifacts or visual clutter that weakens the idea.

## Human authorship
AI assistance does not replace curation. The creator must review the actual final file, confirm the rights basis and approve the exact version before any public release.
