# Operating Scorecard

A lightweight scorecard for a zero-budget, creator-controlled NFT studio. Record only observed facts. Do not turn activity metrics into claims of demand or revenue.

## Weekly review

| Area | Metric | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Art production | New concepts, completed masters, revisions | Repository paths and review notes |
| Quality | Human curation results, technical failures, rework | Quality reports and review records |
| Rights | Works with complete provenance and rights basis | Per-artwork provenance records |
| Audience | Qualified visits, saves, follows and meaningful replies | Native platform analytics |
| Demand | Completed sales, unique buyers, commission inquiries | Source URLs and dated observations |
| Conversion | Inquiries to offers; offers to completed sales | Dated inquiry and settlement log |
| Economics | Gross, platform fees, network fees, refunds, net | Verified settlement records |
| Reliability | CI success rate, failed checks, unresolved defects | GitHub Actions |
| Safety | Open rights, eligibility, security or account risks | Risk register |

## Evidence quality
Label each observation:
- **Verified:** supported by an official transaction, platform export or primary source.
- **Observed:** directly visible but not independently verified.
- **Reported:** supplied by a third party or community post.
- **Hypothesis:** an idea to test, not a fact.

Every market signal should include source, capture date, metric definition and confidence. Do not compare incompatible time windows or count views as buyers.

## 90-day operating rhythm
- **Days 1–14 — Foundation:** complete identity, artwork, provenance, policy, technical and cost gates. No public release until blockers are cleared.
- **Days 15–30 — Validation:** test a small number of organic content themes; collect genuine audience feedback and commission inquiries.
- **Days 31–60 — Iteration:** improve the strongest concepts and presentation using measured evidence; retire weak themes.
- **Days 61–90 — Controlled release:** consider a small release only when all rights, eligibility, fee, owner and technical gates pass.

The schedule is a planning framework, not a promise of sales or audience growth.

## Decision rules
- If there are no qualified signals, improve the work and message before increasing output.
- If engagement is high but inquiries are low, inspect audience fit and the offer.
- If inquiries do not convert, review trust, pricing evidence, rights clarity and checkout friction.
- If costs exceed the zero-budget policy, pause the operation.
- If a metric cannot be verified, label it unknown rather than estimating it.

## Financial reporting
Report separately:
1. Gross sale value.
2. Marketplace and service fees.
3. Network and transaction fees.
4. Refunds and chargebacks.
5. Taxes or withholding, where applicable.
6. Net amount actually received in the wallet or payout account.

An asking price, floor price, listing, offer or unfilled bid is not revenue.
